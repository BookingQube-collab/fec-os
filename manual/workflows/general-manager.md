# General manager — weekly workflow

For **General Managers** responsible for one or more branches: operations health, people, maintenance, compliance signals, and procurement approvals within delegation limits.

## Monday — estate and venue health

1. **Dashboard** — review period chips (Today / This week). Note overdue PMs, critical issues, expiring AMCs/documents, and site readiness.
2. **Sites** — last-30-day league table or heat map; flag underperforming branches.
3. **Command Center → Exceptions** — clear or assign estate-wide items affecting your venues.
4. **Procurement → My Approvals** — GM-tier purchase requisitions (QAR bands per Configuration).

## People and attendance

1. **People → Time & Attendance → Dashboard** — late, missed punches, unmatched User IDs, pending corrections.
2. Approve or reject **Corrections** (not your own).
3. **Import roster** (HR path) when the E3 employee workbook arrives — prefer HR to run **Authoritative Replace**; GMs often use **Safe Sync** at site level where permitted.
4. **Performance** — ensure open evaluation cycle assignments are progressing.

## Maintenance and facilities

1. **Maintenance → Dashboard** and **Requests** — aged requests, technician queue.
2. **Snags** — Kanban for opening defects and contractor follow-up.
3. **Weekly Report** (maintenance) — ensure team reports are submitted before executive review.

## Compliance touchpoints

1. **E3 Tracker → Dashboard** — missing documents and upcoming expiries.
2. **AMC Scheduler** — renewals inside 30 days.
3. **Expiry Alerts** — act before banner escalations.

## Commercial

1. **Revenue** — BookingQube MTD and branch P&L when sync is enabled.
2. **Bookings** pipeline — deposits and confirmed parties for the week.

## Friday — reporting

1. **Weekly Reports (operations)** — submit or review site supervisor forms.
2. **Supervisor Console** — export PDF/Excel for local records if required.
3. **Notifications** — clear assigned approvals and weekly report tasks.
